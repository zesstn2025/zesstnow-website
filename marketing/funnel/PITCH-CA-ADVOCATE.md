# The advocate and CA pitch

Brief: speak as the customer, not the seller. Name their weakness, name their
profit, say nothing about us, then one CTA. Urgency, curiosity, CTA. It must
land as *"I needed this and today it arrived"*, never as *"somebody is selling
me something."*

---

## The mechanism — why this feels like a discovery instead of a sale

A pitch feels like a sale when it makes a CLAIM. It feels like a discovery when
it hands over an OBSERVATION the reader can check for himself in ten seconds
and cannot un-see.

So every message below is built the same way:

1. **An instruction, not a claim.** "Search your own name on Google right now."
   He does it. Whatever he finds, HE found it — we did not tell him anything.
2. **The gap he just saw, named in one line.** No adjectives. No "unfortunately".
3. **His money, in his own arithmetic.** Not our price. What one missed client
   is worth to him, multiplied by his own fee, which he knows better than we do.
4. **Real urgency, never invented.** Nobody is "offering a discount till
   Friday". The honest urgency is that search positions are taken and kept: the
   advocate who put up a page two years ago is the one answering the call
   today, and every month that gap widens. That is true and it is checkable.
5. **A CTA so small that saying yes costs nothing.** Not "book a consultation".
   A one-word reply.

And the rule that makes all of it work: **nothing about us until he asks.** No
company name in the first line, no service list, no years of experience, no
"we are a leading…". The moment a stranger describes himself, the reader knows
he is being sold to and stops reading. We are a person who noticed something.

## What is actually true about these two buyers

Written down because the pitch depends on it being right, not on it sounding
good.

**Advocates — 25,985 on the Allahabad roll.** 16,090 use a gmail or yahoo
address, which means no domain and therefore almost certainly no website.
Their clients used to arrive entirely by reference. That has not stopped, but
a second stream opened alongside it: a young litigant with a property dispute,
a matrimonial matter, a cheque bounce, now types it into a phone first. An
advocate with thirty years of practice and no page loses that stream entirely
to an advocate with three years and a page — not because he is better, because
he is findable.

One property or matrimonial brief in Prayagraj is worth more than the whole
website. He does not need ten clients from it. He needs one.

**CAs.** The arithmetic is different and stronger, and it is the reason a CA is
the better first client of the two. A CA's client is not a transaction, it is a
subscription: GST filed every month, ITR every year, ROC every year. So a
client won once is not worth his first fee — it is worth that fee every year
for as long as the practice lasts. That changes what a website is. For an
advocate it is a lead source. For a CA it is an annuity machine.

And the searches exist: "GST registration Prayagraj", "ITR filing near me",
"company registration Prayagraj". Those are people with money in hand looking
for exactly what he sells, and right now they land on the four or five firms
in the city that put up a page.

## ADVOCATE — e-mail (this is the channel we can send from here)

Subject — a question about him, no offer, no name of ours:

  · `आपका नाम Google पर — एक बात जो शायद आपने देखी न हो`
  · `A question about your name on Google`
  · `Chamber <N> — something worth thirty seconds`

Body:

```
नमस्ते <FirstName> जी,

एक छोटा सा काम कीजिए — अभी, तीस सेकंड लगेंगे।

अपना फ़ोन उठाइए और Google पर लिखिए:
"<अपना नाम> advocate Prayagraj"

अब वही खोजिए जो आपका मुवक्किल खोजता है:
"property case advocate Prayagraj"  या  "criminal lawyer Allahabad High Court"

पहली खोज में शायद आप मिल जाएँ। दूसरी में जो नाम ऊपर आते हैं — क्या वे
आपसे बेहतर वकील हैं? नहीं। वे सिर्फ़ मिल जाते हैं।

आज का मुवक्किल पहले फ़ोन उठाता है, फिर किसी से पूछता है। रेफ़रेंस
बंद नहीं हुआ — उसके साथ एक दूसरा रास्ता खुल गया है, और उस रास्ते पर
आपका नाम नहीं है।

अब अपना हिसाब लगाइए, मेरा नहीं: साल में सिर्फ़ दो मुक़दमे उस दूसरे
रास्ते से आ जाएँ — एक property, एक matrimonial — तो आपकी अपनी फ़ीस
से कितना बनता है? वह रक़म आप जानते हैं, मैं नहीं।

और एक बात सच्ची है: जो वकील दो साल पहले ऑनलाइन आ गए, आज वही फ़ोन
उठा रहे हैं। यह जगह खाली नहीं रहती — भर जाती है, और हर महीने फ़ासला
थोड़ा और बढ़ जाता है।

अगर आप चाहें तो मैं आपके नाम पर एक पन्ना बनाकर आपको दिखा दूँ — बिना
किसी शुल्क के, बिना किसी वादे के। पसंद आए तो आगे बात, न आए तो बात
ख़त्म।

सिर्फ़ "हाँ" लिखकर भेज दीजिए।

— सोनू शर्मा
Zesst Now Services Private Limited
WhatsApp +91 77538 98481
```

**Why each part is there.** The search instruction makes him the one who finds
the gap. "क्या वे आपसे बेहतर वकील हैं? नहीं।" protects his ego instead of
bruising it — the problem is findability, not ability, and that distinction is
what keeps him reading. The money paragraph refuses to name a number and hands
him his own arithmetic, which he trusts because it is his. The urgency is a
fact about how search works, not a deadline we invented. And the ask is one
word, with the exit written next to it.

**Nowhere does it say what we do, what we charge, or how good we are.** That
is the whole design.

## When he replies "हाँ" — what happens next

**Do not send a quote.** A quote turns a conversation back into a sale.

Build the page. One page, his name, his practice areas, his chamber or office
address, his phone. Two hours of work. Send the link with one line:

```
<FirstName> जी, ये देख लीजिए — <link>
कुछ बदलना हो तो बता दीजिए।
```

Now he has something with his own name on it, and it exists. The conversation
about money happens after that, and from a completely different position: he
is not deciding whether to buy, he is deciding whether to keep.

**Then, and only then, the price** — and keep it one number, not a range:

> ₹25,000 — पूरी website, 1 साल hosting, Google पर listing, WhatsApp button.
> ₹6,250 अभी, बाक़ी काम पूरा होने पर.

## The three objections, and the honest answer

**"मेरे पास तो रेफ़रेंस से ही काम आ जाता है।"**
बिल्कुल आता है, और वो बंद नहीं होगा। बात दूसरे रास्ते की है जो अभी पूरा
का पूरा किसी और के पास जा रहा है। Never argue with the referral point — it is
true, and agreeing with it is what earns the right to add to it.

**"इसका फ़ायदा क्या होगा?"**
Do not answer with a number you invented. Turn it back: *एक property brief
आपको कितने का पड़ता है? साल में एक भी आ गया तो?* His number, his conclusion.

**"अभी नहीं, बाद में देखते हैं।"**
The only honest urgency: *जो ऊपर हैं वो हटेंगे नहीं। हर महीने फ़ासला बढ़ता है।*
Then leave. Do not chase twice — the follow-up cadence is 3 days and 7 days,
once each, and then the row closes.

## The part that turns one client into twenty

This is the actual reason advocates and CAs are the first target, and it must
not be forgotten once the first deal lands.

A CA has 150–300 small-business clients. An advocate knows every other
advocate in his chamber corridor. Neither of them is one client — each one is a
door.

So when the work is done and he is happy, the ask is this, on the phone, not
over email:

> *"सर, एक चीज़ माँगनी है — आपके जान-पहचान में 5 ऐसे लोग बता दीजिए जिनकी
> website नहीं है। मैं आपका नाम लेकर बात करूँगा। बस नाम चाहिए।"*

Almost nobody refuses. And the call that follows is not a cold call — it opens
with *"<CA साहब> ने आपका नंबर दिया"*, which is the difference between a 1%
reply rate and a 30% one.

That is the compounding part. The cold list gets you the first client. The
first client gets you the next twenty.
