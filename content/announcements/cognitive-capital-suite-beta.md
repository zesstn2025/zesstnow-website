customers — an AI sales agent that finds your ideal accounts, researches each
one, writes genuinely individual emails, follows up, reads the replies and books
qualified meetings into your calendar.

We are keeping the beta small on purpose so that every account gets set up
properly, particularly the sending infrastructure, which is what decides whether
outbound works at all.

If you are a bootstrapped B2B SaaS founder without a dedicated SDR, or an agency
wanting to offer outbound to your own clients, tell us about your business and we
will let you know when a slot opens.
