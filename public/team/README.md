# Leadership photographs

Three files go in this folder. The names are exact — `components/Leadership.tsx`
checks for these paths at build time and falls back to a monogram if one is
missing, so a typo shows up as a monogram rather than an error.

| File | Person | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sonu-sharma.jpg` | Sonu Sharma | Founder & CEO, Director |
| `rani-devi.jpg` | Rani Devi | Director |
| `nitin-kumar.jpg` | Adv. Nitin Kumar | Legal Advisor |

**Format.** JPEG, portrait, roughly 3:4 — 900×1200 is plenty. The card crops to
3:4 with `object-fit: cover`, so a taller or squarer original still works; it
just crops from the centre. Keep each file under about 400 KB.

**Framing.** Head and shoulders reads best at this size. A full-length photo
will be cropped to the middle of the body rather than the face, so crop it to
the face before committing it.

These are photographs of real people. They must never be replaced with a
generated or stock likeness — the whole point of this section is that the
company is not anonymous.
